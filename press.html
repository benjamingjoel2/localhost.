Boilerplate

Localhost is the event platform built only for tech. Hosts create event pages with speakers, agendas, calls for proposals and sponsor slots, sell tickets in USD, EUR and GBP, and run the door from any phone. Attendees find every tech event in their city in one place and follow the communities behind them. Free for free events; one flat fee on paid tickets. Localhost is live in fifteen cities including San Francisco, New York, London and Berlin.

Facts

  • Founded 2026. Remote team, launching in Berlin, London and San Francisco.
  • Pricing: free events free; 3.5% + 0.30 per paid ticket, card processing included.
  • Currencies: USD, EUR, GBP.
  • Product: event pages, tickets, QR check-in, guest list with role and company, blasts, communities with followers, city hubs, API.
