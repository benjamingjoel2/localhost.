Questions
Is there a plan for communities?

Not yet. Communities, followers, recurring events and co-hosts are free. A Pro tier with custom domains, CRM sync and analytics comes later and is optional.

Which currencies?

USD, EUR and GBP. Buyers pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or SEPA from anywhere.

Refunds?

You set the window. Refunds inside it are one tap for the buyer and the fee is returned too.

Can non-tech events use it?

No. Localhost lists tech events only. That is the point.
