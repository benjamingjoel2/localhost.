we built the event tool we wanted at 2am the night before our own meetup. speakers, a cfp, sponsors, a door that scans, and a guest list that tells you who's actually coming.

how it works

three steps. then you're hosting.

01

pick a type

meetup, hackathon, conference, demo day, workshop, launch party, founder dinner. each one comes with the right blocks: agenda, cfp, tiers, sponsors, teams.

02

share one link

followers get it first. then it lists in your city and monday's email. rich previews in slack, whatsapp, x, linkedin. people see who else is going before they decide.

03

scan them in

any phone is a scanner with a pin. badges print on the spot. blast everyone when doors move. the recording lands on the same link after.

faq

questions people ask.

is it really free?

yes. free events cost you nothing, forever, with unlimited attendees. we make money on paid tickets and sponsor slots.

why tech only?

because discovery only works when the feed isn't polluted. a city page with 97 events is useful when all 97 are for builders.

what about luma and partiful?

great tools, made for everything. neither knows what a speaker, a sponsor or a hackathon team is. we do.

do you have an app?

web first, installable on any phone. tickets and qr work offline. native apps for the scanner and wallet passes come next.
