What is
Localhost?

This is the tool we wanted at 2am the night before our own meetup. A page that knows what a speaker is. A ticket that carries the company name so people can expense it. A door that scans from any phone.

That's what we built at Localhost.

Every event gets a link under its city. Every community gets a home with followers who hear first. Every attendee can see who else is in the room before they decide, and gets the recording if they can't make it.

AI, dev tools, hardware, fintech, design. It doesn't matter what you're building. What matters is that you show up.

Free if it's free. One flat fee if it isn't. See you at the door.
