How it works

Three steps. Then you're hosting.

Pick a template

Meetup, hackathon, conference, demo day, workshop or launch party. Each comes with the right blocks: agenda, CFP, tiers, sponsors. Style it in one tap.

Share one link

Drop it in Slack, WhatsApp, X, LinkedIn or your newsletter. Rich previews, calendar buttons, and a city listing so people who aren't in your network find it too.

Scan them in

Open the door with any phone. Badges print, blasts go out, sponsors see their dashboard, and the recording lands on the same link after.
