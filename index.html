is it really free?

yes. free events cost you nothing, forever, with unlimited attendees. we make money on paid tickets and sponsor slots.

why tech only?

because discovery only works when the feed isn't polluted. a city page with 97 events is useful when all 97 are for builders.

what about luma and partiful?

great tools, made for everything. neither knows what a speaker, a sponsor or a hackathon team is. we do.

do you have an app?

web first, installable on any phone. tickets and qr work offline. native apps for the scanner and wallet passes come next.
